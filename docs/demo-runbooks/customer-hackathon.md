# Customer hackathon runbook

## Goals

- Give teams a safe way to practice prompt-driven Fabric delivery.
- Encourage fast iteration while preserving review discipline.
- Turn prompt output into concrete follow-up stories, not throwaway experiments.

## Best fit

Use this runbook for team-based collaborative sessions where multiple groups explore different persona and scenario paths in parallel.

## Flow

1. Introduce the architecture and the synthetic-data safety boundary.
2. Form teams around persona paths such as data engineering, BI development, or platform governance.
3. Assign each team one scenario prompt pack and one review owner.
4. Have teams refine prompts, compare outputs, and document what Copilot eliminated.
5. End with team readouts that focus on customer outcomes, review gaps, and next actions.

## Roles

- Event lead
- Team coaches
- Review captains
- Outcome judge or steering panel

## Setup

- Share [persona paths](../persona-paths.md) before the session starts.
- Provide direct links to [persona prompt guides](../../prompts/README.md).
- Keep [troubleshooting guidance](../troubleshooting.md) open for prompt tuning.
- Use [placeholder images](../images/placeholders/README.md) if live artifacts are not ready.

## Checkpoints

- Each team uses clear acceptance criteria in its prompt.
- Each team identifies at least one human review correction.
- Each team can explain the business outcome improved by the generated draft.
- Governance and safety constraints remain intact.

## Exit criteria

- Teams leave with prompt patterns they can reuse.
- A shortlist of promising pilot ideas is captured.
- Open admin or licensing dependencies are documented for follow-up.
